# Pine of Glass 0.18.1

A Traceline fix for Ctrl+T.

## Ctrl+T collapses rows opened one at a time

Before this release, Ctrl+T only collapsed rows when Ctrl+O's expand-all was on. A
tool row opened by a click or a drill pin, or a notice opened by a click, stayed open
after pressing Ctrl+T twice, so the transcript did not return to its one-line view.

Ctrl+T now collapses every opened tool and notice row before Pi shows or hides
reasoning. Groups revealed with `▸` and revealed ledgers keep their state, as before.
Ctrl+O can still expand every row when you want them open.
